users
- user_id (UUID, PK)
- username (TEXT, UNIQUE)
- email (TEXT)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- last_login (TIMESTAMP)

campaigns  
- campaign_id (UUID, PK)
- name (TEXT)
- description (TEXT)
- created_by (UUID, FK→users)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- last_played (TIMESTAMP)
- is_active (BOOLEAN)

characters
- character_id (UUID, PK)
- campaign_id (UUID, FK→campaigns)
- user_id (UUID, FK→users)
- name (TEXT)
- class (TEXT)
- level, hp, max_hp, ac (INTEGER)
- stats: str,dex,con,int,wis,cha (INTEGER)
- experience (INTEGER)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
